# PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

## Overview
A web-based, multi-tenant accounting and business management application engineered for SMBs. The platform mirrors core ERP/Accounting workflows (Chart of Accounts, General Ledger, Invoicing, Purchasing, Inventory Control, SARS-compliant VAT tracking, and Financial Statements).

## System Modules
- **Core Accounting & General Ledger:** Double-entry journal engine, CoA, Trial Balance, Period Closing, Financial Reports.
- **Sales & Accounts Receivable (AR):** Quotes, Sales Orders, Tax Invoicing, Credit Notes, Customer Directories, Aged Receivables.
- **Purchases & Accounts Payable (AP):** Purchase Orders, Vendor Bills, Debit Notes, Supplier Directories, Aged Payables.
- **Banking & Cash Management:** Bank Accounts, Statement Imports, Manual Reconciliation, Cashbooks.
- **Inventory & Stock Control:** Multi-warehouse stock tracking, valuation (FIFO/Weighted Average), Adjustments, Stock Takes.
- **Tax & Compliance:** VAT101/VAT201 reporting, Input/Output VAT segregation, Tax Period locking.
- **System Administration & Governance:** User management, RBAC matrices, Document Sequences, Audit Logs.

## Key Target User Workflows
1. **Financial Bookkeeping:** Recording manual journals, maintaining account structures, and balancing ledgers.
2. **Order-to-Cash:** Quote generation $\rightarrow$ Sales Order $\rightarrow$ Invoice $\rightarrow$ Payment capture.
3. **Procure-to-Pay:** Requisition $\rightarrow$ Purchase Order $\rightarrow$ Goods Received $\rightarrow$ Vendor Bill $\rightarrow$ Settlement.
4. **Tax Filing:** Period reconciliation $\rightarrow$ VAT Return calculation $\rightarrow$ Closing journals.
